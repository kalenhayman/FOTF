01 · Partner types

Four ways to work together

Every partnership is written down, with clear responsibilities on both sides.

Technology partners

Registration, scheduling, payments and website platforms. We set up and run your product for mutual clients, so churn drops and support tickets fall.

Service partners

Apparel, photography, facilities, insurance and training providers. We refer clubs that need you, and keep the paperwork moving on both sides.

Associations and governing bodies

Offer FOTF to your member clubs as a vetted admin partner, with reporting that flows back to you.

Referral partners

Coaches, consultants and club directors who know a club that is drowning in admin. Introduce us and we take it from there.
